Questions

What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic. You type to it like a text conversation and it writes back. Our tools are guides that set Claude up to do one job for your business. We're independent and not affiliated with Anthropic.

Do I have to pay for Claude?

No. Every tool works on the free Claude plan. A paid plan gives you more room if you use it a lot.

I'm not good with tech. Can I really do this?

That's who we built these for. The guide walks you through every tap, and if you get stuck you type "I'm stuck" and describe your screen. Still stuck? Reply to your receipt email and we'll help.

Does it send messages or post for me?

No. Everything is a draft. You read it, then you send or post it yourself. Nothing goes out without you.

Is my information safe?

Never paste card numbers, bank account numbers or passwords into any AI. Black out full account numbers on statements before you upload. Your chats live in your own Claude account.

What if it doesn't work for me?

30 day refund. Reply to your Gumroad receipt and you get your money back.
