Build an AI sales agent for your TikTok

Claude learns your product, writes the videos that sell it, scripts your LIVEs, and drafts a reply for every comment and DM in your voice.

TIMEAbout 45 minutes to set up
YOU NEEDYour product details, your FAQs, and Claude
COSTFree
On TikTok, sales come from three things: videos that show the product working, LIVEs, and fast, helpful answers in the comments and DMs. Claude can prepare all three, so you sell more without living in the app.

Step by step

1

Give it your product brain

Paste everything about your product into Claude once:

Here is everything about my product: [what it is, price, sizes, what's included, shipping time, return policy, who it's for, the questions people always ask, and why people hesitate]. Turn this into a one page sales sheet: the main benefit in one line, the top 5 questions with short answers, the top 3 objections with honest replies, and who it's NOT for.
2

Make videos that sell without feeling like ads

Have Claude write a week of product videos in the formats that convert on TikTok:

Using my sales sheet, write 5 TikTok scripts under 25 seconds: 1) the problem, then the product fixing it, 2) a fast demo, 3) three reasons people love it, 4) a reply to a real comment: [paste comment], 5) what's in the box. Each with a 2 second hook and on screen text. Honest, no hype, no claims I can't prove.
3

Script your LIVE

LIVEs sell because people can ask questions in real time. Go in with a plan:

Write a 30 minute TikTok LIVE plan for selling [product]. Include: a 60 second intro that tells people why to stay, when to pin the product, 3 demos, answers to the top questions from my sales sheet, a moment every 10 minutes to welcome new viewers and recap the offer, and a closing. Keep it natural, and remind me to mention if I earn a commission.
4

Answer every comment and DM

Copy a batch of comments or DMs into Claude and get replies in your voice, ready to paste:

Here are new comments and DMs: [paste]. Using my sales sheet, draft a short, friendly reply to each in my voice. Answer the actual question, link to the product only when it helps, and flag any message that needs me personally (complaints, refunds, custom orders).
5

Follow up after the sale

Happy buyers bring the next buyers. Have Claude write the follow up:

Write a short thank you message for people who bought [product], with one tip to get the most out of it, and a polite ask to leave an honest review if they like it. No pressure, and no reward for reviews.
6

Learn from the numbers every week

Paste your weekly sales and video numbers so the agent knows what to repeat:

Here are this week's numbers: [each video or LIVE: views, clicks, orders, top comments]. Tell me which video style and which message sold the most, what questions keep coming up, and what to post next week.

Watch out

  • Never post fake reviews or testimonials, and never offer rewards for positive reviews. The FTC bans fake reviews.
  • If you earn a commission or got the product free, disclose it clearly.
  • Read every reply before you send it. You're responsible for what your account says.
