TikTok Shop lets people buy without leaving the app. There are two ways in: sell your own products as a seller, or earn a commission promoting other people's products as an affiliate creator. Claude helps you pick the right path, do the math and write everything.

Step by step

1

Pick your path

Seller: you list products and get paid for orders. There's no follower minimum; in the US you need to be 18 or older, a US resident, with ID, the last 4 digits of your SSN or an ITIN (or your business documents), a US bank account, and a phone number and email not already used on another shop. Affiliate creator: you earn a commission on products you tag. In the US you can apply at around 1,000 followers and 18 or older. Under 5,000 followers you start in a trial period of at least 30 days, reported as up to 3 shoppable videos a day, 3 shoppable LIVEs a week, no campaigns, and only products from shops in good standing. Requirements change, so check the current rules in the app.

Help me choose between becoming a TikTok Shop seller or an affiliate creator. About me: [followers, what I post, do I have a product or can I get one, budget, time per week]. Compare both for my situation: what I need to start, how I'd make money, the risks, and the first 3 steps for the path you recommend.

2

Pick a product that can win

The best TikTok products are easy to show working in a few seconds. Have Claude pressure test your ideas:

I want to sell on TikTok Shop in [niche]. Give me 10 product ideas that are easy to demo in under 10 seconds, solve a clear problem, are light to ship, and could sell between $15 and $50. 3

Do the math before you list

TikTok takes a referral fee on every order (the rate depends on your category; check it in Seller Center), plus whatever commission you give creators, fulfillment and returns. Know your real profit first:

Build me a TikTok Shop profit table for my product. Price: $[price]. Product cost: $[cost]. TikTok referral fee: [rate from Seller Center]%. Creator commission: [%]. Fulfillment and shipping per order: $[x]. Expected return rate: [%]. Free samples to creators: $[x] each. Show profit per order with and without a creator sale, my break even price, my break even ROAS if I run ads, and the price I'd need for a 30% margin.
4

Write a listing that sells and follows the rules

Paste your product details and let Claude write a clear listing with no claims you can't back up:

Write a TikTok Shop listing for: [product details, sizes, materials, what's in the box]. Give me a title under 100 characters, 5 benefit bullets, a short description, and 3 photo ideas. Only include claims I can prove. Avoid medical, guaranteed result or before and after promises. Flag anything that could break TikTok Shop rules.
5

Get creators to post it

A big share of TikTok Shop sales come from creator videos and LIVEs. Set an open commission in Seller Center's Affiliate Center so any creator can pick up your product, then send personal invites to your top picks:

Write a short message inviting TikTok creators in [niche] to promote my product [product] through TikTok Shop's affiliate program. Mention the commission ([%]), offer a free sample, and give 2 video ideas that fit their style. Keep it short and human, no pressure.
6

Show it every day, the right way

Post product videos that show it working: problem then fix, a quick demo, 3 reasons, or a reply to a real comment. Use real demos with your hands, your face or your own real voice (not AI text to speech), with real movement on screen from the first seconds. TikTok Shop has daily limits on shoppable posts for US accounts. If you post 5 or more static or misleading shoppable videos (slideshows, text only, AI voice, showing one product but linking another) in 7 days, only 7 of your next videos keep their product links for the following week. Check the exact limits in the app. The free Video guide turns this into a weekly plan.

7

Survive your first 30 days

Every new shop starts in a probation period with a daily order cap and a listing cap that rise as you prove you're reliable. Once you have about 30 delivered orders, TikTok gives your shop a Shop Performance Score from 0 to 5 based on product satisfaction, shipping and customer service. Below 3.5 you can't run affiliate plans with creators, and below 3.0 your creator plans are removed, so protecting this score protects your sales. TikTok also tracks an Account Health Rating for policy violations: shops start at 200 points, at 150 you can't add new listings for 7 days, and at 0 the shop can be closed. Check both weekly in Seller Center. So: ship on time with valid tracking, never oversell, answer messages fast, and fix anything that causes returns or bad reviews. When you're ready for ads, GMV Max is the ad type TikTok uses for Shop; start only with a product that's already selling and a budget you can afford to lose.

Make me a first 30 days plan for my new TikTok Shop selling [product]. I can ship within [x] days and spend $[x] on samples and ads. Include: a daily checklist to protect my Shop Performance Score and Account Health Rating (shipping, messages, returns, policy), how many videos to post and what kind, how many creators to contact per day, what to do if a video goes viral and I hit my daily order cap, and when it makes sense to test ads.

Watch out

  • TikTok Shop fees, shipping options, posting limits and eligibility change often. Always confirm in Seller Center and TikTok Shop Academy before you price or launch.
  • Don't chase a viral spike you can't ship. Late or cancelled orders hurt your Shop Performance Score and your probation limits.
  • No claims you can't prove: no cures, no guaranteed results, no fake reviews.
  • Affiliates must clearly disclose that they earn a commission. Use TikTok's disclosure setting and say it in the video too.
