Answering customer messages after hours

Messages that arrive at 7 or 9 PM are often the most ready to buy, and they're the easiest to lose. You don't have to answer instantly, but you do need a system.

Acknowledge fast, answer properly later

A short note you write once ("Thanks, I'm on a job, I'll send your price by 8 AM") keeps the lead warm without you typing a quote at night.

Batch the real replies

Answer everything in one sitting: first thing in the morning and after your last job.

Ask the one missing detail up front

Size, model, address, photos. Whatever you always need before you can price it.

Never promise what you can't check

If you're not sure a time is open, say you'll confirm it.

An example

ExampleExample business
Sorry it's late, can someone come tomorrow for an interior clean? Dog hair everywhere
REPLY
No problem at all! An interior detail is $150, plus $30 for pet hair, about 2 hours. Tomorrow I have 10 AM or 2 PM. Which works?
