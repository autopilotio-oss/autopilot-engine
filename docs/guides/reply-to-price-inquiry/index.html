How to reply to a price inquiry (and win the job)

Most service jobs start with a text that says "how much?". The reply that books the job is short, gives a real number and makes the next step easy.

Give the real price first

A range or "it depends" sends people to the next business. If it truly depends, give the starting price and the one question you need answered.

Say what's included and how long it takes

One line. "About 3 hours, we come to you" answers the next two questions before they're asked.

Offer two specific times

"Saturday at 9 or 1?" is easier to answer than "when works for you?".

End with one question

Ask for the booking, not for more questions.

An example

ExampleExample business
How much for a full detail on an SUV? Free Saturday?
REPLY
Hi! A full SUV detail is $250 and takes about 3 hours. We come to you. I have Saturday at 9 AM or 1 PM. Want me to lock one in?
