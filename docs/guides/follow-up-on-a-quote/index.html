How to follow up on a quote without sounding pushy

A quote that gets no reply isn't always a no. People get busy. A short, friendly follow up wins back jobs that were already gone, and most owners never send one.

Day 2: make it easy to say yes

Repeat the price and offer a time. No pressure.

Day 5: answer the likely objection

Price, timing or trust. Give one reason it's still a good deal, or offer a smaller option.

Last check in: close the loop

"I'll close this out for now, just reply if you'd like to book later." People often answer this one.

Stop after three

Then move on. Following up forever hurts more than it helps.

An example

ExampleExample business
(Quote sent Tuesday: full detail + ceramic, $490. No reply.)
REPLY
Hi Dave, just checking in on the full detail and ceramic coating ($490). Saturday 9 AM is still open, and a $50 deposit holds it. Want me to save it for you?
