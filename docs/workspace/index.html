How it will work

Ten minutes to set up. On your phone.

Connect your line

Your business phone line and your website form email. Planned first: Quo (formerly OpenPhone).

Teach it your business

A 15 question interview: prices, rules, hours, how you talk. You write the one acknowledgment it may send for you.

Approve with a tap

Each lead arrives with the reply and quote drafted. Approve, edit or skip. Follow ups queue themselves.

It always waits for you

  • Every message is logged where you can see it
