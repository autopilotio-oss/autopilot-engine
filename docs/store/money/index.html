Questions

Can I start with one?

Yes. Every kit is $10 on its own, and every one has a free guide you can try first.

Is this an app?

No. It's a PDF prompt kit. You paste the master prompt into a Claude Project once, then run it whenever you need it.

Do I need a paid Claude plan?

No. Claude's free plan works and lets you create up to 5 Projects. A paid plan gives you more usage.

Can it log into my accounts or send things for me?

No. It reads what you upload and drafts. You check it, decide and send.

What about my private info?

Black out full account, card and member numbers before you upload. Claude only needs merchant names, dates and amounts.
