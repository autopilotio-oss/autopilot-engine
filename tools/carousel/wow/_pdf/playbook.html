The idea02

One formula.
Zero templates.

Most grids fail one of two ways. Every post looks the same (a template) or every post looks random (no brand). The Signal Grid fixes both with a short list of fixed rules and a big list of free choices.

Fixed (never changes)
  • 3 colorsblack, paper, one loud accent
  • 1 type systemkicker, headline, footer
  • 1 signal linesame height on every post
  • 1 brand markshows up in many sizes
  • Rows of 3posted together
Free (changes every post)
  • Layoutphoto, type, pattern, card
  • Deviceshard, barcode, pixels, stripes
  • Photowho, crop, mood
  • Connectorhow a row ties together
  • Hookthe words on top
The rule: fixed system, free layouts02 / 10
Anatomy of a row03

Every row has
three jobs.

Left / Hook

A person plus a headline. Stops the scroll and leads into the carousel with the value.

Middle / Pattern

Pure type or pattern. Gives the eye a rest and repeats the idea in a bold way.

Right / Offer

The product. Name, price, what it does, link in bio. Every row sells something real.

Teach, repeat, sell. A visitor sees free value and a clear offer in one glance.

Connectors05

What ties
it together.

  • Signal linea thin stripe at the same height on every post. It flips color with the background.
  • Barcode bridgebars stand on the line and run straight across the cut between two posts.
  • Shardan accent triangle that starts in one post and ends in the next.
  • Sealthe brand mark in a circle, stamped right on the seam.
  • Glitchpixel blocks that spill from a type post into a photo.
  • Baselineevery headline in a row sits on the same height.
Use 2 or 3 connectors per row, never all of them05 / 10

Photo direction07

Clean.
Bright. Sharp.

  • Studioplain grey or white backgrounds. Editorial, young, confident.
  • Black and whitealways. The accent color does all the color work.
  • A gesturehand over face, a headset, headphones, a close up of the eyes.
  • Avoiddark murky shots, sad moods, busy rooms, cheesy stock smiles.
  • Licensefree commercial use only (Unsplash License or your own shots). Save the link with every file.

Content and call to action08

Every slide
earns the swipe.

  • Coverthe hook. One promise in 3 short lines.
  • Middle slidesthe real value, fully visible. No "DM me for it".
  • Last slidethe offer: free option, paid option, one big LINK IN BIO.
  • Single postsstill end with "link in bio" in the footer.
Caption formula

Hook line. What is inside. How to use it. Who it is for, plus the offer. Link in bio. 5 hashtags.

The last slide: two offers and one action.
Value first, offer last, link in bio always08 / 10
Posting playbook09

Drop rows,
not posts.

  • Countyour post count must be a multiple of 3 before a row drops.
  • Orderpost right, then middle, then left. Newest lands top left.
  • Timing3 posts, 5 minutes apart, at your best hour.
  • No singlesanything outside a row goes to stories, not the grid.
  • No pinspinned posts shift the grid.
  • Checkview the row on a phone before it goes live.
