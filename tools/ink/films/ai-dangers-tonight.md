# Ink film 02: AI's real dangers (journalist cut), script draft for Seb's OK

Style: white dot grid paper, black marker, lime highlighter (who/what is real, the fixes) + red highlighter (danger, fakes, money lost), no captions, cut camera, 9:16 column layout.

## Script (about 60 to 70 s, open loop after every answer)
1. HOOK: This man sent twenty five million dollars to his boss. On a live video call. With his coworkers watching. None of them were real. Every face and every voice on that call was made with AI. And that was two years ago. So what is AI doing now?
2. VOICE: Today, about three seconds of your voice is enough to clone it. Most people say they could not tell the difference. Tonight, someone's phone rings and it sounds exactly like their kid. But what if the AI isn't even trying to trick anyone?
3. CONFIDENT LIES: Courts have caught AI made up legal cases in about fifteen hundred rulings. Lawyers fined. One suspended. The AI wasn't lying on purpose. It writes what sounds right, not what is true. So who sees what you type into it?
4. PRIVACY: Last year, thousands of shared ChatGPT chats showed up on Google. Some with names and personal problems. One checkbox. And when AI makes decisions about you?
5. BIAS AT SCALE: A major hiring software company is in court, accused of letting its AI reject people for age, race and disability. One bad rule, run on millions of applications. So how big is this, really?
6. TONIGHT: The FBI logged eight hundred ninety three million dollars in AI linked fraud last year. That is about two point four million dollars a day. Reported. The real number is higher.
7. WHAT TO DO: Pick a family safe word. Confirm any money request on a second channel. Keep private data out of chatbots. Check every name, number and source.
8. CLOSE: AI isn't evil. It just made faking things almost free. Don't confuse confidence with truth. (ap. logo) Part two tomorrow.

## Fact sheet (checked Nov 8 2026)
- Arup, Hong Kong, early 2024: employee sent HK$200M (about US$25.6M) in 15 transfers after a video call with deepfaked CFO and staff; Arup confirmed fake voices and images were used. Fortune, May 17 2024.
- Voice cloning: McAfee test, 3 seconds of audio gave an 85% voice match; 70% of adults not confident they could tell a clone; 77% of victims lost money (7,054 people, 7 countries). McAfee via Help Net Security, May 2023.
- AI hallucinations in court: about 1,490 decisions worldwide, over 1,000 in the US, as of May 2026 (Damien Charlotin database, via gc.ai). Sixth Circuit sanctions Mar 2026; Nebraska suspension Apr 2026.
- Privacy: nearly 4,500 shared ChatGPT conversations found in a Google site search, some with names and sensitive details; OpenAI removed the discoverable option. Fast Company, Jul 30 2025.
- Bias: Mobley v. Workday (N.D. Cal.), alleges AI screening discriminated by race, age and disability; agency theory survived dismissal, case in discovery. Allegations, not findings.
- FBI IC3 2025 report: 22,364 AI related complaints, $893M adjusted losses ($893M / 365 = about $2.4M a day, reported only); total cybercrime losses $20.877B. SecureWorld, Apr 9 2026.
- Deepfake losses recorded worldwide: $2.5B in 2025, $764M in H1 2026, $3.7B since 2020 (Surfshark, Jul 14 2026; AI Incident Database, Resemble.AI, OECD).
