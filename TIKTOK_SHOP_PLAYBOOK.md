## 1. How TikTok Shop actually works (US)
- Two ways to earn: **Seller** (you list products, ship orders, get paid per order) or **Affiliate creator** (you tag other sellers' products and earn commission). A brand can also run its own **Official Shop account** (no follower minimum, only its own products).
- **Seller eligibility:** 18+, US resident, ID plus SSN/ITIN last 4 (individual) or US business documents (business). No follower minimum. Register at seller-us.tiktok.com. [VERIFY yearly]
- **Affiliate eligibility:** independent Affiliate Creators need at least 1,000 followers, 18+, US based, identity verification and no revoked e-commerce permissions on linked accounts. Accounts under 5,000 followers enter the Affiliate Pilot for at least 30 days with limits (see 1b). Marketing and Official Shop creators have no follower minimum. (Verified 2026-11-04 on TikTok Shop Academy "Creator Eligibility Policy", updated 09/25/2026. Some blogs still quote 5,000 to apply: that is the graduation number, not the entry number.) [VERIFY quarterly]
- **Fees:** a referral fee per order (6% was the standard US rate for most categories; several third party sources report 8% for most non food categories since Aug 4, 2026, but TikTok's public category table, last revised May 2026, still shows 6%, and 5% for some jewelry and pre-owned; rechecked 2026-11-04, still no official confirmation of 8%). Plus creator commission (commonly 10 to 20%), fulfillment, returns. **Never quote a fee as fact: tell people to check their category's rate in Seller Center.** [VERIFY]
- **Fulfillment (CORRECTED 2026-11-04):** the plan to end independent Seller Shipping for US sellers (announced Jan 2026, deadlines Feb 9 to Mar 31) was called off in Feb 2026: TikTok told sellers "Seller Shipping remains unchanged, and previously shared deadlines are not going into effect" (Modern Retail/Digiday, Feb 19 2026; Easyship, Feb 23 2026). US sellers can ship themselves, use a 3PL, or use Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT). Our earlier line saying seller shipping had ended was wrong; it was never in a free guide or kit. [VERIFY quarterly]
- **Probation (new shops):** every new shop starts with a daily Order Volume Limit and a listing cap that step up (reported tiers: Beginner about 50 orders/day and 100 listings, Standard about 100/200, Premium about 200/1,000, then Pro with no cap). When the cap is hit, products show as out of stock until the daily reset. Graduation depends on on-time shipping, low seller-fault cancellations, low non-buyer-fault returns and low negative review rate. [VERIFY numbers]
- **Shop Performance Score (SPS):** 0 to 5, based on product satisfaction, fulfillment and logistics, customer service, benchmarked against your category. It gates features (affiliate access, campaigns, badges, settlement speed, cheaper return shipping above 4). Aim for 4+. Separate from the **Account Health Rating (AHR)**, a 0 to 1,000 compliance score that replaced violation points from July 2026; since Aug 17, 2026 some violations deduct more points (Policy Pulse Aug 2026). [VERIFY]
- **Ads:** GMV Max is the only campaign type for TikTok Shop ads (since July 2025). It optimizes to GMV with a daily budget and ROI target and uses your organic, affiliate and ad videos. Use it to scale a product that already sells profitably, not to find one.
- **Posting limits:** since May 11, 2026 US accounts have daily caps on shoppable posts (reported about 30 shoppable videos and 60 shoppable photo posts per day for healthy accounts, lower for new or weak ones). Posting 5+ "non-interactive" shoppable videos (static slideshows, text only, no human or demo) within 7 days can trigger a Content Posting Limit where only a few videos keep product links. [VERIFY numbers]
- **Content and listing rules:** revised Content Policy (May 22, 2026) and Product Listing Policy (June 2, 2026): stricter on claims, especially in LIVE. No medical claims, no guaranteed results, no misleading before/after, nothing you can't prove. AI-generated realistic people, voices or scenes must be labeled; no AI likeness of real people without permission. [VERIFY]
- **Disclosure:** affiliates disclose commission or free product clearly (FTC Disclosures 101) and use TikTok's disclosure setting. The FTC rule (effective Oct 21, 2024) bans fake reviews, bought reviews and fake social indicators.

## 1b. Rules added after the Oct 31 independent review (all [VERIFY] quarterly)
- **Reviews (TikTok Shop Review Policy, June 26, 2026):** ask every buyer, once per order, through TikTok Shop messages. Never ask only happy buyers, never ask twice, no rewards outside TikTok's official program, no package inserts asking for (positive) reviews (packaging rules, Sept 2026). FTC rule: no fake reviews, no rewards tied to positive reviews.
- **Real humans only:** AI voices and playback narration are banned in shopping LIVEs and reportedly in promotional content (US Academy, May 23, 2026). Shoppable videos need real moving footage from the start (no still images or loops). Teach "your hands, your face or your own real voice (not AI text to speech)".
- **Content Posting Limit:** 5+ non-interactive or misleading shoppable videos in 7 days triggers a 7-day limit where only 7 videos keep product links. Daily caps on shoppable posts since May 11, 2026 (numbers not officially published; trial affiliates up to 3 shoppable videos a day).
- **SPS gates creators:** appears after about 30 delivered orders in 90 days; below 3.5 no affiliate plans, below 3.0 existing creator plans removed.
- **Account Health Rating:** starts at 200 (scale 0 to 1,000). 150 = no new listings and no new mega campaign enrollment for 7 days; 100 = 14 days; 50 = 28 days; 0 = the shop can be permanently deactivated. Milestone quizzes can shorten the 150/100/50 restrictions (AHR Requirements doc, June 25 2026, via PPC Land June 29 2026; rechecked 2026-11-04).
- **Affiliate trial (Affiliate Pilot):** at least 30 days, up to 3 shoppable videos a day, 3 shoppable LIVEs a week, no campaigns, only products from shops with a high Shop Performance Score (the policy says "95% or higher"); graduate after 30 days and 5,000 followers. Accounts tagged **Extended Pilot** (abnormal status) get 3 shoppable videos and 1 shoppable LIVE a week and can't graduate until status improves. Early restriction removal is at TikTok's discretion: pass the probation quiz, keep Creator Health Rating at 176+, and do one of 6+ shoppable videos of 8+ seconds, a 5+ minute LIVE with Shop products, or 10 orders from shoppable content. Scheduled, posted, deleted and removed shoppable videos all count toward the cap; drafts don't. (Academy: Creator Eligibility Policy 09/25/2026; Affiliate Creator Pilot Program Introduction 05/13/2026; verified 2026-11-04.)
- **LIVE:** usually about 1,000 followers to go LIVE; 18+ to sell; live speech only.
- **Privacy:** strip buyer names, addresses, phones and order numbers before pasting into AI; never contact buyers off TikTok.
- **Search:** put search words in the caption, the on screen text and say them in the first seconds.
- **Scheduling reality:** Metricool free plan about 20 scheduled posts a month; schedulers usually can't add Shop product links or trending sounds, so shoppable videos are posted from the app.
- **Sound:** TikTok is a sound-on platform (TikTok/Kantar: 88% say sound is essential); captions still help reach and search. Only commercial-library sounds for anything sold.
- **Before stocking a product:** check TikTok Shop's Prohibited Products Policy; set an open commission in Affiliate Center, then send personal invites.

## 1c. Changes found 2026-11-04 (weekly intel)
- **Creator Health Rating (CHR):** creators have their own 0 to 1,000 score, start at 200 (green 200+, orange 150 to 199, red under 150). At 150, 100 or 50 they can temporarily lose shoppable posting or campaign access; at 0 they lose all TikTok Shop creator permissions. Points come back through compliant shoppable content that completes orders or a 100% policy quiz. CHR 2.0 (Policy Pulse Sept 2026) changed how points are earned, lost and recovered; details not public outside Seller Center. Sellers: shops linked to creators who lose e-commerce permissions may be restricted from adding creators (Policy Pulse July 2026). Sources: Hubfluence May 12 2026; Academy Policy Pulse. [VERIFY]
- **AI listing images (Policy Pulse Sept 2026):** new restrictions on AI generated product listing images. Trade press (soona, July 2026) reports AI made models and fabricated scenes need a visible AI label; light edits (cleanup, background removal) are treated as normal editing. Teach: real photos of the real product; check the rule in Seller Center before using any AI image. [VERIFY exact rule]
- **Listings and returns (Policy Pulse Jul to Sept 2026):** low quality assortment products can lose visibility (Sept); TikTok now manages returns sent to FBT warehouses (Sept); return addresses must be owned or authorized business addresses, effective Aug 5 2026, or To Ship orders can be cancelled; never market a product as "CPSC approved, certified or tested" (Aug); "GLP-1" style terms in titles or branding are prohibited (July); forward shipping fees are no longer refunded on buyer responsible returns (July).
- **Returns option:** UPS Happy Returns is live on TikTok Shop (Retail Dive, Oct 20 2026): box free, label free returns at about 10,000 US locations. FBT orders need no setup (activate in Seller Center if already a Happy Returns user); seller fulfilled shops can set it up through Happy Returns (pitched at roughly 10,000+ returns a year).
- **Holiday window:** TikTok Shop US Black Friday and Cyber Monday campaign runs Nov 11 to 30, 2026 (TikTok Shop press release, GlobeNewswire Oct 19 2026). Eligible products can show a 3-Day Delivery badge. TikTok says US LIVE Shopping sales more than doubled year over year in H1 2026, and LIVE sellers grew BFCM 2025 weekend sales 84% (TikTok's own figures, not ours; never repeat as a promise).
- **GMV Max:** still the Shop ads type. Reported Oct 5 2026 (Advertising Week NY, via Ecomdy): GMV Max will factor affiliate commissions, coupons and referral fees into optimization, no rollout date. GMV Max Pro (ROI that counts those costs) was in testing from May 2026. [VERIFY before teaching]
- **Fees:** no official change found this week; 8% still unconfirmed by TikTok (see section 1).
- **No change found this week:** posting limits and the Content Posting Limit (5+ non interactive or misleading shoppable videos in 7 days -> only 7 linked videos for 7 days, BigSeller Mar 5 2026), probation tiers, SPS thresholds, LIVE follower rule, FTC disclosure and fake review rules. No new FTC action specific to TikTok Shop found in the last 7 days.

### 3 tactics that work now (Nov 2026)
1. **Plan LIVEs into Nov 11 to 30.** TikTok's own data puts LIVE at the center of BFCM. Book 2 to 3 short shopping LIVEs a week in the window with a run of show; trial affiliates remember the 3 LIVEs a week cap.
2. **Seed samples narrow, then double down.** Send small sample batches to creators whose videos already convert, give each video 1 to 2 months, stop seeding non converters, and move budget to proven creators (higher commission, Spark/GMV Max on their best video with permission). Source: TikTok Shop Playbook newsletter (2025, still consistent with 2026 practice).
3. **Win the delivery promise before the spike.** Ship fast enough for the 3-Day Delivery badge where eligible, make returns easy (Happy Returns for FBT), and keep stock below your order volume limit so a viral video doesn't turn into late shipments and a lower SPS.

### 3 tactics that work now (week of Nov 11 2026)
1. **Use the BFCM shipping extension as a buffer, not a plan.** Ship as if you had no extra days: Late Dispatch Rate over 20% blocks campaign registration and SPS under 3.0 can remove benefits mid campaign, and a spike you can't ship costs more than it earns.
2. **Pick words from Compass Search rankings.** Take the trending search terms for your category from Compass and put the exact words in the caption, on screen text and the first spoken line (our search rule in 1b), then check which videos pick up search traffic.

## 2. The pro operating system
1. **Pick products like a pro:** visible result in under 10 seconds, solves a clear problem, light and hard to break, roughly $15 to $50, enough margin for 10 to 20% creator commission AND ads AND returns. Kill anything in a restricted category or that needs a health claim to sell.
2. **Do the math first:** profit per order for organic, creator and ad sales; break-even price; break-even ROAS. If a creator sale loses money, the product isn't ready.
3. **Protect the score from day one:** ship fast with valid tracking, never oversell, reply to messages fast, fix the root cause of every return and 1-star review. A viral spike you can't ship is a loss.
4. **Content engine:** real demos with hands, faces or voice. Proven formats: problem then fix, fast demo, 3 reasons, reply to a real comment, unboxing, comparison. Hook in the first 2 seconds (spoken and on screen). Quality over volume: 1 to 3 strong shoppable posts a day beats 20 slideshows.
5. **Creator engine:** most GMV comes from affiliates. Target creators whose audience matches, offer a fair commission and a free sample with 2 specific video ideas, follow up, track who posts and what sells, double down with the winners (extra samples, higher commission, Spark/GMV Max on their best video with permission).
6. **LIVE:** a run of show (60 second opener with a reason to stay, product pins, demos, recaps every 10 minutes, honest offers only), compliant claims, repeat disclosures.
7. **Scale with ads only after proof:** GMV Max on a product with organic and creator sales, a test budget you can lose, a realistic ROI target.
8. **Weekly review:** GMV, orders, conversion, top videos and creators, returns and reasons, SPS and AHR, posting-limit warnings. Decide: double down, fix, cut, one test.
