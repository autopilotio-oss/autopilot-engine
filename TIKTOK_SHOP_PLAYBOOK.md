## 1. How TikTok Shop actually works (US)
- Two ways to earn: **Seller** (you list products, ship orders, get paid per order) or **Affiliate creator** (you tag other sellers' products and earn commission). A brand can also run its own **Official Shop account** (no follower minimum, only its own products).
- **Seller eligibility:** 18+, US resident, ID plus SSN/ITIN last 4 (individual) or US business documents (business). No follower minimum. Register at seller-us.tiktok.com. [VERIFY yearly]
- **Affiliate eligibility:** independent Affiliate Creators can apply at about 1,000 followers, 18+, US based. Accounts under 5,000 followers enter a creator trial ("pilot") period of at least 30 days with limits (for example, which shops they can promote). [VERIFY: sources disagree, some still quote 5,000]
- **Fees:** a referral fee per order (6% was the standard US rate for most categories; several sources report 8% since Aug 4, 2026). Plus creator commission (commonly 10 to 20%), fulfillment, returns. **Never quote a fee as fact: tell people to check their category's rate in Seller Center.** [VERIFY]
- **Fulfillment:** since March 31, 2026, independent seller shipping is reported as ended for US sellers; orders go through Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) or TikTok's shipping programs. [VERIFY]
- **Probation (new shops):** every new shop starts with a daily Order Volume Limit and a listing cap that step up (reported tiers: Beginner about 50 orders/day and 100 listings, Standard about 100/200, Premium about 200/1,000, then Pro with no cap). When the cap is hit, products show as out of stock until the daily reset. Graduation depends on on-time shipping, low seller-fault cancellations, low non-buyer-fault returns and low negative review rate. [VERIFY numbers]
- **Shop Performance Score (SPS):** 0 to 5, based on product satisfaction, fulfillment and logistics, customer service, benchmarked against your category. It gates features (affiliate access, campaigns, badges, settlement speed, cheaper return shipping above 4). Aim for 4+. Separate from the **Account Health Rating (AHR)**, a 0 to 1,000 compliance score that replaced violation points from July 2026. [VERIFY]
- **Ads:** GMV Max is the only campaign type for TikTok Shop ads (since July 2025). It optimizes to GMV with a daily budget and ROI target and uses your organic, affiliate and ad videos. Use it to scale a product that already sells profitably, not to find one.
- **Posting limits:** since May 11, 2026 US accounts have daily caps on shoppable posts (reported about 30 shoppable videos and 60 shoppable photo posts per day for healthy accounts, lower for new or weak ones). Posting 5+ "non-interactive" shoppable videos (static slideshows, text only, no human or demo) within 7 days can trigger a Content Posting Limit where only a few videos keep product links. [VERIFY numbers]
- **Content and listing rules:** revised Content Policy (May 22, 2026) and Product Listing Policy (June 2, 2026): stricter on claims, especially in LIVE. No medical claims, no guaranteed results, no misleading before/after, nothing you can't prove. AI-generated realistic people, voices or scenes must be labeled; no AI likeness of real people without permission. [VERIFY]
- **Disclosure:** affiliates disclose commission or free product clearly (FTC Disclosures 101) and use TikTok's disclosure setting. The FTC rule (effective Oct 21, 2024) bans fake reviews, bought reviews and fake social indicators.

## 1b. Rules added after the Oct 31 independent review (all [VERIFY] quarterly)
- **Reviews (TikTok Shop Review Policy, June 26, 2026):** ask every buyer, once per order, through TikTok Shop messages. Never ask only happy buyers, never ask twice, no rewards outside TikTok's official program, no package inserts asking for (positive) reviews (packaging rules, Sept 2026). FTC rule: no fake reviews, no rewards tied to positive reviews.
- **Real humans only:** AI voices and playback narration are banned in shopping LIVEs and reportedly in promotional content (US Academy, May 23, 2026). Shoppable videos need real moving footage from the start (no still images or loops). Teach "your hands, your face or your own real voice (not AI text to speech)".
- **Content Posting Limit:** 5+ non-interactive or misleading shoppable videos in 7 days triggers a 7-day limit where only 7 videos keep product links. Daily caps on shoppable posts since May 11, 2026 (numbers not officially published; trial affiliates up to 3 shoppable videos a day).
- **SPS gates creators:** appears after about 30 delivered orders in 90 days; below 3.5 no affiliate plans, below 3.0 existing creator plans removed.
- **Account Health Rating:** starts at 200; 150 = no new listings for 7 days; 0 = shop can be closed.
- **Affiliate trial:** at least 30 days, up to 3 shoppable videos a day, 3 shoppable LIVEs a week, no campaigns, only shops in good standing; graduate after 30 days and 5,000 followers.
- **LIVE:** usually about 1,000 followers to go LIVE; 18+ to sell; live speech only.
- **Privacy:** strip buyer names, addresses, phones and order numbers before pasting into AI; never contact buyers off TikTok.
- **Search:** put search words in the caption, the on screen text and say them in the first seconds.
- **Scheduling reality:** Metricool free plan about 20 scheduled posts a month; schedulers usually can't add Shop product links or trending sounds, so shoppable videos are posted from the app.
- **Sound:** TikTok is a sound-on platform (TikTok/Kantar: 88% say sound is essential); captions still help reach and search. Only commercial-library sounds for anything sold.
- **Before stocking a product:** check TikTok Shop's Prohibited Products Policy; set an open commission in Affiliate Center, then send personal invites.

## 2. The pro operating system
1. **Pick products like a pro:** visible result in under 10 seconds, solves a clear problem, light and hard to break, roughly $15 to $50, enough margin for 10 to 20% creator commission AND ads AND returns. Kill anything in a restricted category or that needs a health claim to sell.
2. **Do the math first:** profit per order for organic, creator and ad sales; break-even price; break-even ROAS. If a creator sale loses money, the product isn't ready.
3. **Protect the score from day one:** ship fast with valid tracking, never oversell, reply to messages fast, fix the root cause of every return and 1-star review. A viral spike you can't ship is a loss.
4. **Content engine:** real demos with hands, faces or voice. Proven formats: problem then fix, fast demo, 3 reasons, reply to a real comment, unboxing, comparison. Hook in the first 2 seconds (spoken and on screen). Quality over volume: 1 to 3 strong shoppable posts a day beats 20 slideshows.
5. **Creator engine:** most GMV comes from affiliates. Target creators whose audience matches, offer a fair commission and a free sample with 2 specific video ideas, follow up, track who posts and what sells, double down with the winners (extra samples, higher commission, Spark/GMV Max on their best video with permission).
6. **LIVE:** a run of show (60 second opener with a reason to stay, product pins, demos, recaps every 10 minutes, honest offers only), compliant claims, repeat disclosures.
7. **Scale with ads only after proof:** GMV Max on a product with organic and creator sales, a test budget you can lose, a realistic ROI target.
8. **Weekly review:** GMV, orders, conversion, top videos and creators, returns and reasons, SPS and AHR, posting-limit warnings. Decide: double down, fix, cut, one test.
