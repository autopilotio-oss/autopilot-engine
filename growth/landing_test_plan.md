One variable changes per variant (the promise and the demo it opens on). Price, refund, plan, limits, FAQ and checkout are identical.

## Tracking per variant
- Visit: GoatCounter path (`/front-desk/after-hours/`), UTM kept in session storage.
- Checkout intent: GoatCounter event `checkout-click/autopilot/<page>/<source>` (and Meta `InitiateCheckout` once the pixel exists).
- UTMs travel into the Gumroad link (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, `ft`), verified in QA.
- Email signup: Kit form 10118407 with `fields[source]=frontdesk` and the UTMs; GoatCounter event `optin/fd_<variant>`. The Kit welcome sync task routes `frontdesk` to sequence 2951307.
- Purchase: ledger `order_paid` with the UTMs Gumroad returns (to be confirmed by the paid test in `tests/e2e_purchase.md`).
- Ad links must carry `utm_medium=paid_social` explicitly; without it the site labels a source as `organic_social`.
